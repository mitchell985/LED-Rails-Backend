Te Huia movement modelPrototype

Te Huia has no realtime feed, so its LED is computed from the timetable: where it should be between two scheduled station times. Only three Auckland times exist — The Strand, Puhinui, Pukekohe — spread over 50.8 km and 62 track blocks. This page lets you drive that model by hand.

The question: does distance-proportional interpolation over legs that long look plausible on the board, and does the rule that Te Huia yields to real trains behave sensibly — or does it oscillate, deadlock, or teleport?

Two knobs below are undecided questions, not settled design. Dwell has no published source (ticket 13) — the feed says zero. Clear blocks is ambiguous in the map itself: the decision was labelled “one clear block between them” but described as “at most the block behind the occupied one”. Try both and see which looks right.
